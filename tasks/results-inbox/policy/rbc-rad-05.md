RBC-RAD-05 Follow-up of radiology recommendations

Applies to radiology reports that contain a follow-up recommendation, whoever ordered the study. The pulmonary nodule table is a local rule modelled on the Fleischner Society 2017 guidelines, which are older guidance.

§1 Intervals
Follow-up intervals in a report run from the date of the index study, not from today.

§2 Incidental solid pulmonary nodule smaller than 6 mm
Low risk (fewer than 20 pack-years and quit 15 or more years ago, or never smoked): no routine follow-up; document the decision.
High risk (20 pack-years or more, or quit less than 15 years ago): CT chest without contrast 12 months after the index study, window plus or minus 1 month.

§3 Urgent recommendations
A recommendation marked urgent, or a suspected malignancy, with no stated interval: order within 14 days of the report date, priority urgent.

§4 Tracking
Track every follow-up order with a Task due at the end of its window.
