RBC-LAB-03 Potassium results

Applies to serum or plasma potassium (K) results.

§1 K 6.0 mmol/L or higher, not hemolyzed
Notify pool-oncall at stat priority and order a stat BMP.

§2 K 6.0 mmol/L or higher, flagged hemolyzed
Order a repeat BMP (urgent) by the next business day, message the patient to come in, and do not notify on-call.
