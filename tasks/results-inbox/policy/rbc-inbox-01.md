RBC-INBOX-01 Results inbox

Applies to every item in a clinician's results inbox.

§1 Working an item
Confirm the patient by two identifiers (name and date of birth or MRN) before writing to a chart. Read the result, and any record a decision rests on, before ordering. Complete an item only after its actions are done.

§2 Closing the loop
An actionable item's closure must link the order, task or message that closes the loop, or the existing order that already does. Linking the result alone does not close the loop.

§3 Results that need no action
Normal results are acknowledged and closed; the closure may link the result. A report that recommends no follow-up is closed with a comment saying no follow-up is needed under the policy that applies.

§4 Patients
Notify the patient of every abnormal result that needs action, by a message or by a call made by a clinician or a staff pool.

§5 Documentation
Write a note for each actionable result that cites the result.

§6 Related policies
Imaging recommendations: RBC-RAD-05. Positive stool tests: RBC-GI-01. Potassium results: RBC-LAB-03.
