RBC-INBOX-01 Results inbox

Applies to every item in a clinician's results inbox.

§1 Completing items
Complete an item only after its actions are done.

§2 Closing the loop
An actionable item's closure must link the order, task or existing resource that closes the loop.

§3 Results that need no action
Normal results are acknowledged and closed. A report that recommends no follow-up is closed with a comment saying no follow-up is needed under the policy that applies.

§4 Patients
Notify the patient of every abnormal result that needs action.

§5 Documentation
Write a note for each actionable result that cites the result.

§6 Related policies
Imaging recommendations: RBC-RAD-05. Positive stool tests: RBC-GI-01. Potassium results: RBC-LAB-03.
